## 1. Boundary Between Human Creativity & AI Assistance

### Human-Originated Concept, Innovation & Engineering Decisions (Team RoboBee Innovators)
- **Mission Concept ("Be Your Own Doctor" in Deep Space)**: Designed around long-duration missions (e.g., Mars transit) where limited cabin space, prolonged Earth isolation, and zero onsite hospital/physician mean astronauts need a personal preventive, physical, and behavioral health companion.
- **NASA Historical Routine Baseline**: Team decision to inspect short/ISS NASA human spaceflight data to establish realistic daily astronaut schedules (work blocks, exercise routines, sleep hours, free time) and baseline human limits before projecting onto long missions.
- **Individual Astronaut Profiles & Continuous Wearable Concept**: Defined the multi-sensor wearable layout (pulse oximeter MAX30102 for SpO2 & heart rate, 3-electrode adhesive ECG setup, gyroscopic/IMU sensor for sleep/movement, OLED readout, compact ESP controller with USB/Wi-Fi telemetry) plus an interactive animated wearable prototype directly inside the web application.
- **Pragmatic Camera Pivot ("Astronaut Interview" & Visual Care via Laptop Webcam)**: Following reliability issues with external camera hardware (HuskyLens), the team decided to make the standard laptop webcam the primary sensor for astronaut facial recognition login, stress/emotion check-ins during voice/text interviews, visual injury/cut inspection, and multi-day wound recovery checks (with HuskyLens kept only as optional hardware).
- **Futuristic 3D Interactive Astronaut UI Concept**: Team UX design replacing static tabular dashboards with a faceless/skinless translucent wireframe 3D human body at the center of the portal that displays only the beating heart and major arterial/venous pathways, rotates continuously, supports both autonomous AI camera choreography and manual mouse orbit, and animates perspective-correct poses (*e.g.*, demonstrating how to hold an injured finger in front of the camera).
- **"Hi Lunar" Wake-Word Companion & 3-Way Affect Cross-Check**: Team-designed voice wake-word assistant (**Lunar**) that identifies the crew member automatically via webcam face recognition, passively observes facial emotion (calm, happy, tense, sad, fatigued) from the start of the conversation without manual recording toggles, and cross-checks spoken self-reports against facial affect and wearable vitals.
- **Never-Forgets Adaptive Companion & Autonomous Report Walkthrough**: Team product design sequencing weekly/daily reports automatically on screen: explaining every Heart Rate peak/trough, correlating nocturnal SpO2 drops during sleep with matching NASA OSDR/NTRS incident/study records, auditing skipped exercises, scanning and marking finger cuts on camera with healing assessment, and prescribing next-week circadian sleep windows, diet adjustments, and injury-safe workouts.

### Planned AI-Assisted Scope (Updated Per Prompt As Files Are Built)
- **Coding Assistant Used**: Antigravity AI coding assistant (workspace software scaffolding, boilerplate generation, UI component styling, and documentation structuring strictly supervised by the team).
- **Deterministic Science Separation**: Sensor calculations, Z-score anomaly math, Pulse Arrival Time (PAT) cuffless blood pressure estimation (`ECG R-peak` + `PPG peak`), workout rules, and follow-up timers will reside in deterministic code outside LLM prompts so AI never fabricates vitals.
